## Verdict

1. **Approve with changes.** The architecture (D1–D24) is implemented coherently and with a rare depth of fact-checking; money (`WireAmount`, round-trip, `split` = `distributeAmount`) and cutover/rollback are worked out better than average.
2. Before the start of execution close the 2 Blockers: the SW scope in 04-2d before the cutover (C2-01) and offline deletion/editing of a settlement through the outbox (C2-02).
3. The Major findings are mostly "an agent without context will get stuck": contradictions between specs (C2-03/04/06/07), unassigned work (C2-05/08/19), names before definition (C2-08/09/10), manual inputs (C2-11/17/18).
4. Top-5 risks:
5. (1) An offline regression in prod before the cutover from a literal execution of §2.11 in 04-2d (C2-01).
6. (2) A violation of D2 "payments never offline" through the common `deleteEntry`/PATCH (C2-02).
7. (3) Integrity of the outbox queue: cancelling dependent operations and ordering by wall clock (C2-15/16); 03-15 is too large for one session, which raises the chance of error (C2-12).
8. (4) A red CI by construction: `warn` against `--max-warnings 0`, no baseline before 02-C6, an async selector without an owner (C2-03/04/05).
9. (5) Parallelism is overestimated: freeze windows, an incomplete file-lock, codemods against store migrations (C2-13/14); the real critical path of offline is 11+ sequential PRs.
10. After the fixes of C2-01…C2-20 the package can be handed to agents by PR id; the Minors can be fixed along the way in the corresponding PRs.
